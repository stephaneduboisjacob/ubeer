# Promotional Kit

These are copy drafts for the uBeer brand and sales conversations. They are not proof that a particular city is live, that a product is available, or that any delivery or payment claim applies in every country. Replace bracketed fields and confirm every claim for the target region before publishing. Alcohol advertising, age targeting, sponsorship, discounting, and influencer rules differ by location; obtain local review.

## Brand position

**Working positioning:** A mobile-first local marketplace that helps eligible adults discover products available in their delivery area, check out through a hosted crypto-payment flow, and follow order progress in one place.

**Voice:** clear, useful, confident, neighborhood-minded, adult, and responsible. Avoid language that encourages excessive drinking, targets minors, promises instant delivery, or implies alcohol improves health, confidence, status, or performance.

## Short descriptions

**One line:** uBeer brings local beverage ordering, hosted crypto checkout, and delivery updates together in one mobile-friendly experience.

**Short app description:** Check whether your address is in a served area, browse the local menu, pay through BTCPay checkout, and follow order status from your phone. Availability, age checks, and payment methods depend on your location.

**Long description:** uBeer is built around local service areas. Enter an exact address to see whether delivery is available, review the current catalog and fees, and continue to a secure hosted checkout. Follow order progress from your phone and complete the handoff with the driver. The customer and driver experience is designed for mobile browsers, with an Android app shell and installable web app. Service is for eligible adults only and is offered only in configured locations where local rules permit it.

## Feature bullets

- Address-based service-area check before shopping.
- A catalog and delivery fee tied to the configured region.
- Hosted BTCPay checkout; customers see the payment options enabled for that store.
- Order-status updates and a delivery handoff code.
- Customer, driver, and operations views in a mobile-friendly platform.
- Interface language choices include English, French, Italian, Spanish, Simplified Chinese, and Russian; market-specific translation and legal review are still required.

## Claim controls

**Twenty-four-hour delivery:** Use “We deliver 24 hours per day” only when the local operator is staffed and legally permitted to accept, prepare, and deliver orders at every advertised hour. Otherwise replace it with “Delivery hours vary by area.” Never use this claim for the current prototype without confirming operating hours and local rules.

**More than 2,000 cryptocurrencies:** The brand has used a “2,000+ cryptocurrencies” promotional claim. Publish it only after verifying that the specific BTCPay store, enabled integrations, network availability, and checkout flow really offer the advertised assets to customers in that region. Prefer: “Pay using the methods available at secure checkout.” The checkout page is the definitive list.

**Coverage and speed:** Name only active, staffed regions. Do not promise a delivery time unless the operator can support it and the rules allow the wording. An address check is not a guarantee that stock or a driver is available.

## Ready-to-adapt campaign messages

**Launch post:** “Meet uBeer in [approved service area]. Check your address, explore the local menu, and follow your order from your phone. Available to eligible adults where local rules permit. See the app for current products, fees, hours, and payment options.”

**Waitlist message:** “Not in a uBeer delivery area yet? Leave your email on the uBeer site and we’ll share an update if service reaches [area]. Joining the list is not an order or a promise of launch.”

**Driver recruitment:** “Interested in local delivery work in [area]? Review the requirements and apply through the uBeer Driver page. Applications are reviewed before driver access is granted. Local permits, eligibility, and vehicle requirements apply.”

**Partner introduction:** “uBeer provides a region-gated ordering and delivery workflow with customer, driver, and administrator views. We are looking for licensed local operators and product partners in [market] to assess service coverage, catalog, fulfillment, and compliance before launch.”

## Visual and publishing checklist

Use the current artwork as prototype brand material, not proof of an active local operation. Do not show minors, unsafe consumption, driving after drinking, or unverified partner logos. Add the local responsible-use line, age gate, support link, and service terms to each campaign landing page. Confirm translated copy, prices, taxes, delivery hours, product imagery rights, and platform availability before publication. Maintain a record of the reviewer, market, approved wording, and review date.
