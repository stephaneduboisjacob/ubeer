# Driver Guide

## Before you apply

Driver work is available only in an active region configured by the operator. Review local rules for alcohol delivery, required permits, minimum age, licensing, insurance, vehicle use, and identity checks before applying. The current form asks for your full name, phone, email, vehicle, license number, preferred region, and an ID file in JPEG, PNG, or PDF format up to 5 MB.

Submit the form at the Driver app page. Submission is not approval. The operator reviews the application and documents. Keep your contact details reachable; the prototype does not guarantee an email notification because email transport may still be configured for console output.

## Sign in after approval

An administrator approves or rejects the application. When approved, the admin screen creates a driver account and displays a temporary password once. The administrator should send it through a secure channel. Sign in at Driver app with the application email and temporary password. The current app has no self-service password-reset screen; contact the platform operator if you cannot sign in. Never reuse the temporary password elsewhere.

## Go online and receive work

Open the app on your phone and allow location access while online. Tap Go online only when you are ready and legally able to perform deliveries. The app sends the browser's location about every 30 seconds while it is online; the backend uses the latest point to determine whether you can receive an order in your assigned region. Turn off online status when you stop working, and close or sign out of the app when finished.

Assignments refresh about every ten seconds. An eligible paid order can be assigned to an approved online driver in the same region whose reported location is within that region. Review the address, order reference, and customer contact shown in the assignment. Use Open delivery location in maps to open OpenStreetMap directions. The current app is not a turn-by-turn navigation service and does not show the customer a live driver map.

## Complete an order

Follow the in-app progression: paid → accepted → preparing → out for delivery → delivered. Accept only work you can complete safely. If you cannot take a newly assigned paid order, use Decline; the system can offer it to another eligible driver. Follow the operator's pickup and handoff procedure, check that the customer is legally eligible to receive alcohol, and request any ID required by local rules.

At the handoff, ask the customer to show the order's six-digit delivery code. Enter it only in the delivery action in the app. The server checks the code before allowing delivered status. Never ask the customer to send it remotely, record it outside the platform, or mark an order delivered before handoff. Contact the operator if a customer cannot provide the code or the recipient is not eligible.

## Earnings and payment

The driver screen shows recorded earnings and a platform-fee percentage. Earnings are calculated from delivered orders using the configured platform fee. The dashboard is an estimate/record, not a pay statement or guarantee of settlement. Driver payouts are entered and marked paid manually by an administrator; the app does not transfer funds. Agree payment timing and method with the operator before taking assignments, and check each payout record with them.

## Safety and privacy

Keep the ID and delivery documents you see private. Do not photograph, download, or retain customer documents unless a lawful process requires it. Use customer information only to complete the assigned delivery. Do not share a customer's address, email, or order details. If you have an accident, security concern, suspected intoxication, or delivery dispute, stop the handoff and contact the operator through the approved channel. Follow local alcohol, road-safety, and employment rules at all times.
