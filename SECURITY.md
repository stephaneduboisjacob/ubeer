# Security Policy

uBeer can process customer addresses, driver applications, order details, and payment events. Treat address data, identity documents, credentials, database dumps, and production logs as sensitive.

Do not report suspected vulnerabilities in a public issue. Contact the maintainer privately at [jacobstephane@outlook.com](mailto:jacobstephane@outlook.com) with a concise description and safe reproduction steps. Do not attach real customer data, credentials, or identity documents.

This prototype does not establish alcohol licensing, age-verification, tax, privacy, or delivery compliance. Do not use it for real orders without the required legal, security, and operational reviews.

Contact: Stephane Jacob <jacobstephane@outlook.com>
